Ultimate Sanctuary: credits and licences

Back to Ultimate Sanctuary

Every recording is used under a Creative Commons licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution needed, credited anyway) or CC BY (attribution, as given here). Recordings were found on Freesound. Where a recording was changed, the change is described. The synthesised doctors (the warm hearth drone, the ancient winds, the deep earth rumble, the primordial shallow sea and all the brainwave tones) are made inside the app and use no recordings.

The app code was written for Ahsan Ali, who may use, change and sell it freely.
